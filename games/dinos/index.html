Volcano Escape

Work together to get all three dinosaurs to the Safe Island before the volcano erupts!

How many players?

Your turn: Roll the die. A number lets you move any one dinosaur that many spaces. Tap a dino, then tap one of the two glowing squares.

The 🌋 face: no move, but the volcano grows. After 5 volcano rolls it erupts and everyone loses.

Peek a chip: When a dino stops on a green, rocky, or mountain land, flip one fern chip there. It shows for a moment — remember it!

Rescue: Get a dino to its own picture chip and flip it to send that dino to the Safe Island. 🦖 the T‑Rex sends the dino back to its start. Fossils & bugs do nothing.
