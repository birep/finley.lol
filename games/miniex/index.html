RIPPA R10 TRAINER

A faithful practice rig for the R10 mini-excavator controls — built from the operator's manual. Learn the ISO joystick pattern, the dozer lever, travel levers & pedals, throttle and starter sequence before you climb onto the real machine.

Starts in external view — press C (or FIRST-PERSON) to climb into the seat. Drag a lever to operate it, or use the keyboard. Right-drag (or two-finger) looks around.

SIM ONLY · NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR CERTIFIED TRAINING
